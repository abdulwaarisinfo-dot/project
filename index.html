FAQ

Questions store owners actually ask

Still unsure about something? Here's the short version.

How long does setup actually take?+
1–3 days on Express, 3–5 days on Standard, and up to 7 days on Basic — depending on how much catalog and brand detail you give us in the setup form.
Do I need to give up my Shopify or WooCommerce admin access?+
No. Cartmind connects through the platform's own app/API permissions — it reads your catalog, stock, and order status, and never needs your login.
What happens after the free 7-day trial?+
Nothing automatic. If you don't upgrade, the assistant simply pauses — no card is charged, and no auto-renewal kicks in.
Can the assistant reply in more than one language?+
Yes. Basic includes one language, Standard up to two, and Express supports unlimited languages — Urdu, English, Arabic, and more.
Is this allowed under WhatsApp's rules?+
Yes. Cartmind stays scoped to your own business data — catalog, orders, and support — which keeps it compliant with WhatsApp's 2026 messaging policy.
Can I switch plans later?+
Yes, upgrade or downgrade anytime. We only re-run the parts of setup that change, not the whole process.
